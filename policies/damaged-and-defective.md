# Damaged and defective items

## Arrived damaged
If an item arrives broken or damaged, the customer is eligible for a full refund within the 30-day return window. Return shipping is free for damaged items.

## Keep it, no return needed
For damaged items under $50, the customer doesn't need to send the item back. We refund it and they can recycle or discard it.

## Photos
For damaged items over $50, we may ask for a photo of the damage and the packaging. A photo is never required to start the conversation.

## Return shipping for everything else
For returns that aren't damaged or defective (changed your mind, wrong size, didn't like the color), return shipping costs a flat $7.95, deducted from the refund.
