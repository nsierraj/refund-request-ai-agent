# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 7 business days after the order ships. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.

## Processing time
Orders usually ship within 2 business days. Large furniture can take up to 5 business days to ship.

## Lost or late packages
If tracking hasn't updated for 10 days, or the package is marked delivered but the customer can't find it, the support team opens a trace with the carrier. Agents should not refund lost packages directly; hand them to a team member.
