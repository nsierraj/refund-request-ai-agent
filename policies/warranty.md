# Warranty

## What's covered
Electrical items (lamps, espresso machines, kettles, small appliances) have a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. It covers defects in parts and workmanship, not accidental damage.

## Warranty vs. refund
Within the first 30 days, a defective item can be refunded under the normal return policy. After 30 days, a defect is handled as a warranty claim: the item is repaired or replaced, not refunded.

## How to start a warranty claim
The customer needs the order ID and a short description of the problem. A support team member then sends repair or replacement instructions.
