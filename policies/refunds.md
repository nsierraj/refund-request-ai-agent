# Refunds

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method. We can't send a refund to a different card or as cash.

## How long refunds take
Once a refund is issued, it usually shows up on the customer's statement in 1 to 3 business days, depending on their bank.

## Refunds that need a human
Any refund over $1500 is reviewed by a member of the support team before it's issued. The review usually takes 1 to 2 business days. The customer gets an email with the decision.

## One refund per order
An order can only be refunded once. If a refunded order has a new problem, the customer should contact support so a team member can look at it.
